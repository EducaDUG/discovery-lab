Accessibility, built in at engine level

  • — Every control reaches 44px and takes keyboard focus with one consistent ring.
  • — Correct/incorrect always carries a glyph, never colour alone.
  • — Four text sizes, five background tints, dyslexia-friendly face, reduced motion.
  • — Nothing in the system flashes or strobes. No animation exceeds 2Hz.
  • — Default ground is warm paper, never pure white.
