🐋 Cuvier's Deep Dive

Ocean Zones & Habitats — pilot the deepest-diving mammal on Earth

You are a Cuvier's beaked whale. Breathe at the surface, then dive through the sunlight, twilight and midnight zones toward the seabed — nearly 3,000 metres down. Hunt deep-sea squid with suction feeding, dodge orcas, sharks and fishing gear, and answer the ocean each zone asks of you by swimming to real marine features — coral heads, fish schools, hydrothermal vents and rock formations — that carry the possible answers.

W / S — pitch nose down (dive) / up (rise)
A / D — turn left / right
Shift — boost (burns more breath)
F — hold near prey to suction-feed
Q — go quiet (evade sonar & predators)
R — confirm a chosen answer with a body roll

🫁 Surface regularly to breathe. 🔊 Echolocation switches on automatically below ~400 m to reveal glowing prey. 🎯 A question appears in the water just before every reward — swim to the correct feature, then roll to lock it in. Answer all 10 to complete the dive. 🌡️ Watch your instruments: temperature, salinity, pressure and dissolved oxygen all change as you descend.
