💧 Day Zero: Reservoir Manager

Keep Rivelago's reservoir alive for 12 years — or watch it run dry

You manage the reservoir that supplies Rivelago: a farming valley, a growing town and a factory all draw from the same water. Rainfall is falling as the climate changes. Every year, set how much each sector may draw, decide which conservation measures to fund, then advance the year and watch the reservoir respond — in real time, in 3D.

🖱️ Drag to orbit the valley, scroll (or the +/− buttons) to zoom. 🖱️ Click the dam, farms, town, factory or wetland to inspect them. Some are hiding a quick check of your understanding. 🎯 Reach Year 12 with water still in the reservoir to win — let it hit zero, and the city faces Day Zero.
